\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Hyperbolic Representation and Indexing.}
{Poincar\'e}, {Lorentz}, and mixed-curvature embeddings model hierarchies and entailment~\cite{nickel2017poincare,sala2018representation,ganea2018hyperbolic,ganea2018entailment,tifrea2019poincare,nickel2018lorentz,gu2019mixed} in graph learning~\cite{chami2019hgcn,chami2020low}, vision and language~\cite{chen2022fully,khrulkov2020hyperbolic,ermolov2022hyperbolic,desai2023meru}, and retrieval~\cite{madhu2026hyprag,lewis2020rag}.
Metric trees and navigating nets~\cite{yianilos1993data,krauthgamer2006navigating} face ball overlap in high dimensions. Wu and Charikar~\cite{wu2020hyperbolicnn} group points by radius and search each band in Euclidean space. Hyperbolic {LSH}~\cite{deng2026hyperboliclsh} probes many tables at high recall, and {HyEm}~\cite{deng2026hyem} uses origin log-maps and Euclidean {HNSW}.
Native graphs~\cite{boytsov2013engineering,prokhorenkova2022hyperbolic,glukhota2026hyperspacedb} use exact coordinates or ambient quantization. Worst-case navigable degree can grow as $\Omega(n)$ without Steiner points~\cite{deng2026navigable}. \rquiver{} separates conformal scalars from directional codes and assigns edges by geometry.

\paragraph{Graph Topology and Locality.}
{NSW}/{HNSW}~\cite{malkov2014approximate,malkov2020hnsw}, {NSG}/{NSSG}~\cite{fu2019nsg,fu2022high}, and {Vamana}/{DiskANN} and its extensions~\cite{subramanya2019diskann,singh2021freshdiskann,chen2021spann,gollapudi2023filtered} underpin flat {ANN} benchmarks~\cite{aumuller2020annbenchmarks,wang2021comprehensive,simhadri2022results,azizi2025graph}.
{RoarGraph}~\cite{chen2024roargraph} uses query logs to address cross-modal hub occlusion. Theorem~\ref{thm:vamana_pathologies} shows how curvature changes same-shell pruning around an origin neighbor, motivating the angular links in {QuadriPrune}.
{Starling}~\cite{wang2024starling} reorders vertices to pack SSD blocks. \texttt{HotSmPage} groups each vertex's hot state within one in-memory page.

\paragraph{Low-Bit Quantization.}
{PQ}/{OPQ} and their {SIMD} variants~\cite{jegou2011pq,ge2013opq,andre2015cache,andre2017accelerated}, hyperbolic {PQ}~\cite{qiu2024hihpq}, and anisotropic {MIPS} quantizers~\cite{guo2020accelerating,sun2023soar} learn codebooks.
Training-free methods include {LVQ}/{LeanVec} and 1-bit {BBQ}~\cite{aguerrebere2023similarity,tepper2024leanvec,trent2024bbq}, binary hashing~\cite{indyk1998approximate,charikar2002similarity}, and rotation-based {RaBitQ}/{SymphonyQG}~\cite{gao2024rabitq,gao2025extendedrabitq,gou2025symphonyqg}. Han et al.\ (AISTATS 2026) recursively quantize Euclidean polar angles after random preconditioning~\cite{han2026polarquant}.
For anisotropic embeddings~\cite{wang2020understanding,betser2026infonce}, \rquiver{} extends prior unrotated 2-bit {SM} and {MNR} analysis~\cite{xiao2026quiver,xiao2026decisions} to conformal weighting, tangent-cone residuals, and hyperbolic graph construction.

\paragraph{Vector DBMSs.}
Vector engines~\cite{pan2024survey}, including {Faiss}, {Milvus}/{Manu}, {PASE}, {AnalyticDB-V}, {VBASE}, and {SingleStore-V}~\cite{johnson2019billion,douze2024faiss,wang2021milvus,guo2022manu,yang2020pase,wei2020analyticdb,zhang2023vbase,chen2024singlestore}, use flat $L_2$/IP search. \rquiver{} provides a native hyperbolic operator with a proxy that preserves geodesic order.
