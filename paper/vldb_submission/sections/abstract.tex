Million-scale nearest-neighbor search in hyperbolic space must preserve geodesic order across radial shells while limiting the data fetched at each graph hop. Negative curvature permits interior hubs to occlude same-shell neighbors and amplifies directional errors near the boundary.

We present \textbf{\rquiver{}}, a static graph index with in-memory navigation for the Poincar\'e ball and Lorentz hyperboloid. It separates FP32 radial scalars from two-bit directional codes, prunes edges by radial and angular roles, co-locates navigation data in $4$\,KiB pages, and reranks a short candidate list in FP64.

On native {MERU-1M}, \rquiver{} serves $2{,}547$/$53{,}483$ queries per second at $99.25\%$ Recall@10, or $1.97\times$/$3.37\times$ the throughput of {HyperspaceDB} FP32 flat at $99.03\%$ recall ($1$/$24$ threads). On mapped {Cohere-1M}, navigation occupies $517$\,MB versus $3{,}373$\,MB for {Polar-FP32}. Including exact reranking brings their accounted index totals to $3{,}589$ and $3{,}373$\,MB. On the public-query frontier at $99\%$ recall, \rquiver{} serves $42{,}466$ queries per second on 24 threads versus $7{,}004$ for {Polar-FP32}.
