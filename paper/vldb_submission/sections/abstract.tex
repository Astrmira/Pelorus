Hyperbolic embeddings in the {Poincar\'e} ball and {Lorentz} hyperboloid capture hierarchies with low distortion, yet million-scale approximate nearest neighbor ({ANN}) search faces a fidelity--memory dilemma: flat inner-product proxies distort geodesic order across radial shells, whereas native hyperbolic graphs fetch full-precision vectors per hop or quantize ambient coordinates without conformal decoupling.
Transplanting flat {Vamana} pruning and low-bit codes also fails because inward-bending geodesics let interior hubs occlude spherical neighborhoods, conformal weights $(1 - c\|x\|_2^2)^{-1}$ break cross-shell query-noise cancellation, and tight boundary cones suffer margin-to-noise ratio ({$\MNR$}) collapse.

We present \textbf{\rquiver{}}, an in-memory warped-product graph index pairing a 32-byte unquantized FP32 radial/conformal header with scale-invariant 2-bit Sign-Magnitude ({SM}) spherical bitplanes and tail FP64 reranking.
\rquiver{} attenuates intra-cone tangent error at zero per-vertex bytes via purity-gated tangent-cone residuals with Conformal Jacobian gating (\textsc{CJ-Gate}), constructs fiber-bundle graphs via inside-out \emph{QuadriPrune} and degree-preserving chord swaps (achieving full directed {BFS} reachability from entry hubs across all measured graphs), and co-locates headers, stabilized 256-bit sketches, and bitplanes in $4096$\,B pages scored by a 3-to-2 carry-save {AVX2} kernel and orthogonal-projection refinement.

Across eight {AMD Zen~4} benchmarks, \rquiver{} accelerates {Cohere-1M} builds by $5.44\times$ over {Polar-FP32} ($1.91\times$ over 8-bit {Polar-SQ8}), cuts hot navigation memory to $517.0$\,MB page-co-located ($2.44\times$ below {Polar-SQ8}, or $485.0$\,MB / $2.60\times$ split), lifts {Cohere-1M} $99\%$-recall QPS by $4.30\times$/$6.31\times$ (1T/24T) over {Polar-FP32}, and reaches $53{,}483$\,QPS on {MERU-1M} ($3.37\times$--$3.88\times$ over {HyperspaceDB} FP32) on the global 2-bit path ($0$ cones on diffuse real sets), while activating $256$ cones on clustered shells to raise {Clustered-Narrow} recall from $34.22\%$ to $99.385\%$ at $L=256$ ($99.80\%$ at $L=384$; $1.84\times$--$4.56\times$ over {HyperspaceDB}) and delineating recall limits on isotropic narrow shells.
