\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Separating FP32 radial scalars from two-bit directions gives \rquiver{} a compact traversal working set at high recall on native MERU-1M. Cold vectors can reside on SSD. Residual coding improves recall on deep WordNet, while limited query-time coverage constrains search on isotropic narrow shells.
