\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textbf{\rquiver{}}, a warped-product graph index for hyperbolic search on $\Bball$ and $\Lmod$.
Factoring points into unquantized FP32 radial/conformal scalars ($32$\,B header) and scale-invariant 2-bit {SM} codes keeps directional error out of boundary denominators while compressing logical state to $384$\,B/vertex ($517.0$\,MB page-co-located at $N=10^6, D=768$, $2.44\times$ below {Polar-SQ8}; $352$\,B / $485.0$\,MB / $2.60\times$ split).
Combining {QuadriPrune}, chord-swap {BFS} repair, radial-minimizer routing, $4096$\,B \texttt{HotSmPage} co-location, orthogonal-projection refinement, and purity-gated tangent-cone/\textsc{CJ-Gate} recovery yields $5.44\times$ faster builds than {Polar-FP32} ($1.91\times$ vs.\ {Polar-SQ8}), $4.30\times$/$6.31\times$ (1T/24T) higher $99\%$-recall {Cohere-1M} QPS, and $53{,}483$\,QPS on {MERU-1M} ($3.37\times$--$3.88\times$ over {HyperspaceDB} FP32) on the global path ($0$ cones across diffuse real sets), while activating $256$ cones on clustered shells to lift {Clustered-Narrow} from $34.22\%$ to $99.385\%$ ($L=256$; $99.80\%$ at $L=384$) and delineating degree-and-budget limits on isotropic {Uniform-Narrow} ($64.735\%$ at $16/128$ vs.\ $92.17\%$ for {HyperspaceDB} at $32/256$).
