\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Representation models that embed text, images, and structured entities into negatively curved {Riemannian} manifolds---specifically the {Poincar\'e} ball $\Bball$ and the {Lorentz} hyperboloid $\Lmod$---provide a principled geometric inductive bias for hierarchical retrieval.
Because geodesic ball volume in hyperbolic space grows exponentially with radius rather than polynomially, trees, ontologies, and asymmetric entailment cones embed with far lower metric distortion than in flat {Euclidean} space~\cite{nickel2017poincare,sala2018representation,ganea2018entailment,gu2019mixed}.
Recent encoders scale curved representations across graph neural networks~\cite{nickel2018lorentz,chami2019hgcn}, fully hyperbolic Transformers and vision backbones~\cite{chen2022fully,khrulkov2020hyperbolic,ermolov2022hyperbolic}, million-vector multimodal alignment ({MERU}~\cite{desai2023meru}), and hyperbolic dense retrieval for retrieval-augmented generation ({HypRAG}~\cite{madhu2026hyprag,lewis2020rag}).

Serving low-latency, high-recall approximate nearest neighbor ({ANN}) queries over curved manifolds nevertheless remains a severe database systems bottleneck.
In flat {Euclidean} and cosine spaces, proximity graphs such as {HNSW}~\cite{malkov2020hnsw}, {NSG}~\cite{fu2019nsg}, and {Vamana} ({DiskANN})~\cite{subramanya2019diskann,azizi2025graph} define the latency--recall frontier.
Industrial and academic compressed graph engines---including locally-adaptive scalar quantization ({LVQ})~\cite{aguerrebere2023similarity}, binary lookup tables ({BBQ})~\cite{trent2024bbq}, randomized extended codes ({SymphonyQG})~\cite{gou2025symphonyqg}, and training-free 2-bit Sign-Magnitude ({SM}) indexing in {\quiver}~\cite{xiao2026quiver}---further accelerate search by reading full FP32 vectors only during tail reranking.
In hyperbolic spaces, existing approaches force an unsatisfactory trade-off.
Data-oblivious hyperbolic locality-sensitive hashing ({LSH})~\cite{deng2026hyperboliclsh} requires wide multi-table probing at $99\%+$ recall, while flattening $\Bball$ or $\Lmod$ into {Euclidean} or inner-product proxies inside production vector databases ({Faiss}~\cite{johnson2019billion,douze2024faiss}, {Milvus}~\cite{wang2021milvus}, {AnalyticDB-V}~\cite{wei2020analyticdb}, {VBASE}~\cite{zhang2023vbase}) conflates radial hierarchy depth with angular proximity and inflates candidate pools across radial shells.
Conversely, evaluating exact geodesic distances over full-precision coordinates at every graph hop~\cite{boytsov2013engineering,prokhorenkova2022hyperbolic,glukhota2026hyperspacedb} incurs heavy compute and memory stalls: near the manifold boundary ($\|x\|_2 \to 1/\sqrt{c}$), the conformal factor $f_x = 2/(1 - c\|x\|_2^2)$ diverges and requires IEEE-754 double-precision accumulation to prevent catastrophic cancellation, while random $3\text{--}6$\,KB vector fetches across millions of vertices amplify random memory traffic and data {TLB} ({DTLB}) pressure.

\subsection{Why Flat Graph Indexing Breaks Under Negative Curvature}
\label{subsec:intro_obstructions}

How should proximity-graph indexes be architected for negatively curved manifolds?
Geometrically, $\Bball$ is not an isotropic vector space but a \emph{warped product} $\R_{\ge 0} \times_{\frac{1}{\sqrt{c}}\sinh(\sqrt{c}R)} \Sunit$ coupling a radial depth half-line with an exponentially expanding sphere.
Our theoretical and diagnostic study reveals that transplanting flat single-metric {Vamana} pruning~\cite{subramanya2019diskann} or flat compressed navigation~\cite{aguerrebere2023similarity,gao2024rabitq,xiao2026quiver} onto this warped product fails due to three coupled geometric obstructions:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Cross-Shell Conformal Non-Cancellation and Lorentz-MIPS Blowup.}
  In flat cosine search, ranking candidates $x$ and $y$ against query $q$ evaluates $\langle u_q, u_x\rangle - \langle u_q, u_y\rangle$; because both terms weight $u_q$ equally, first-order quantization noise on $u_q$ cancels identically as $u_x \to u_y$~\cite{xiao2026decisions}.
  In $\Bball$, the monotonic geodesic proxy weights each directional inner product $\langle u_q, u_v\rangle$ by a candidate-dependent conformal factor $W(r_q, r_v) = 2 r_q r_v f_q f_v = \frac{2}{c}\sinh\rho_q\sinh\rho_v$.
  Whenever $r_x \ne r_y$, query quantization leaves an uncancelled residual scaled by $W(r_q, r_y) - W(r_q, r_x) \ne 0$ that grows exponentially with boundary depth.
  Worse, quantizing $(D+1)$-dimensional {Lorentz} coordinates directly via Minkowski inner products multiplies coordinate quantization noise by $\|\tilde{z}\|_2 = \Theta(e^\rho)$ without separating radial norms.

  \item \textbf{Radial Occlusion Bounds and Angular Escape Conditions.}
  Hyperbolic geodesics bend inward toward the origin $0 \in \Bball$.
  We prove that if an outer vertex $u$ at geodesic depth $R_u$ admits an interior neighbor $p$ ($R_p < R_u$) during {Vamana} \textsc{RobustPrune}~\cite{subramanya2019diskann}, then \emph{every} candidate inside the ball $R_v \le (R_u - \alpha R_p)/(\alpha + 1)$ satisfies $\alpha\,d_{\mathbb{B}}(p, v) \le d_{\mathbb{B}}(u, v)$ simultaneously across all spherical angles $\theta_{pv} \in [0, \pi]$---even $180^\circ$ opposite $p$---while along any outer shell $\rho = \sqrt{c}\,R$, negative curvature shrinks the same-shell angular cone surviving origin pruning exponentially as $\sin(\theta_{uv}/2) < \sinh(\frac{\alpha}{2}\rho)/\sinh(\rho) \sim e^{-(2-\alpha)\rho/2}$ (from $73.7^\circ$ in flat space to $23.1^\circ$ at $\rho = 4$ for $\alpha = 1.2$).
  Interior hubs consequently shadow lateral boundary links and isolate $518\text{--}626$ zero-in-degree boundary islands per million vectors on {Cohere-1M}; removing directional protection in our controlled three-seed {Cohere-1M} ablation drops $99\%$-recall throughput by $5.26\times$ ($2{,}071 \to 394$\,QPS) and prevents reaching $99.5\%$ recall.

  \item \textbf{Boundary Cluster Margin Collapse, Pool Dilution, and the Rotation Paradox.}
  When semantic subtrees form tight angular cones near the boundary ($\alpha_v = \langle u_v, \mu_c\rangle \to 1$), intra-cluster angular differences shrink proportionally to $1 - \alpha_v^2$, while global coordinate-wise 2-bit {SM} noise remains $\Theta(1/\sqrt{D})$.
  As the comparison margin-to-noise ratio ({$\MNR$}) collapses toward zero, expanding beam width $L$ under a fixed rerank budget $B$ counterintuitively \emph{reduces} recall by flooding the candidate pool with indistinguishable intra-cluster distractors: on a 1M-vector clustered narrow-shell benchmark, beam traversal visits $93.9\%$ of true top-$10$ neighbors at $L=256$ yet retains only $49.3\%$ in the finalist beam and $33.0\%$ after pool gating ($34.2\%$ across all $2{,}000$ queries), and raising $L$ from $256$ to $2{,}048$ lowers recall monotonically from $34.2\%$ to $27.8\%$.
  Global random orthogonal rotations~\cite{gao2024rabitq,ge2013opq} cannot serve as a general remedy because rotating structured real embeddings destroys native coordinate covariance and shared-endpoint cancellation~\cite{xiao2026decisions}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Warped-Product Polar Co-Design in \rquiver{}}
\label{subsec:intro_solution}

We address these obstructions with \textbf{\rquiver{}}---named after the maritime bearing dial that decouples angular direction from radial course---an in-memory hyperbolic proximity-graph index grounded in \emph{warped-product polar decoupling}.
Every point $x \in \Bball$ (and via exact stereographic isometry every point $z \in \Lmod$) is factored into exact FP32 radial scalars $(r_x, f_x, \rho_x)$ stored in a 32-byte header and a scale-invariant directional code on the unit sphere $\Sunit$, instantiated via training-free 2-bit {SM} bitplanes.
Keeping radial scalars exact prevents quantization error from ever entering the singular conformal denominator $1 - c r_x^2$ and decouples hyperbolic distance into exact radial lower bounds plus weighted spherical inner products.

From this representation, \rquiver{} co-designs conformal polar estimation primitives, fiber-bundle topology, and page-aligned hardware execution:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Conformal Polar Estimation and Tangent-Cone Recovery (\S\ref{sec:theory}):}
  We prove that polar decoupling makes candidate flip probabilities completely invariant to query boundary proximity ($f_q \to \infty$), derive a deterministic zero-parameter \emph{orthogonal-projection asymmetric estimator} (\textsc{Stage-0.5}) based on exact two-level {SM} Pythagorean reconstruction, establish $1$-strong convexity of the hyperbolic-cosine objective and derive its closed-form \emph{radial minimizer} along directional rays, and introduce a purity-gated \emph{local tangent-cone residual code} paired with an off-axis \emph{Conformal Jacobian plateau gate} (\textsc{CJ-Gate}) that restores intra-cluster {$\MNR$} with zero per-vertex byte overhead while leaving diffuse real embeddings untouched.

  \item \emph{Warped-Product Fiber-Bundle Topology (\S\ref{sec:design}):}
  Rather than pruning edges under a single isotropic predicate, \rquiver{} inserts vertices inside-out by geodesic depth and partitions each out-degree list across four geometric channels via \emph{QuadriPrune}: stem-excess geodesic fibers, radially shielded \emph{isobathic} (same-shell) rings where $W(r_q, r_x) \approx W(r_q, r_y)$ restores shared-query cancellation, and step-aligned inward and outward radial elevators, followed by a linear-time spanning-tree chord-swap pass that eliminates all zero-in-degree islands and smoothly recovers flat spherical {Vamana} as $c \to 0^+$.

  \item \emph{Page-Aligned Polar Execution (\S\ref{sec:system}):}
  To eliminate the dual {DTLB} walks of Structure-of-Arrays layouts, \rquiver{} co-locates each vertex's $32$\,B polar header, binomial-variance-stabilized 256-bit early sketch, and complete 2-bit {SM} bitplanes inside a power-of-two record within a single $4096$\,B OS page ($16$ vertices/page at $D=768$), evaluates surviving 5-popcount bitplane products via a 3-to-2 carry-save {AVX2} kernel, refines the converged beam in L1 cache via fixed-point masked subset sums, and reranks the top tail in IEEE-754 FP64 {SIMD}.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Contributions.}
Our main contributions are summarized as follows:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Building on flat comparison-decision theory~\cite{xiao2026decisions}, we analyze conformal polar quantization and hyperbolic {Vamana} pathologies on $\Bball$ and $\Lmod$, characterizing query boundary-depth invariance, cross-shell query-noise non-cancellation, Lorentz-MIPS quantization blowup, radial occlusion with exponential angular-cone contraction, fixed-budget pool dilution, deterministic orthogonal-projection de-biasing, and zero-byte-per-node tangent-cone {$\MNR$} recovery (\S\ref{sec:theory}).
  \item We design a warped-product graph construction and routing pipeline combining inside-out geodesic seeding, stem-excess {QuadriPrune} degree partitioning, spanning-tree chord-swap island repair ($1{,}000{,}000 / 1{,}000{,}000$ directed reachability), and routing via the constrained radial minimizer (\S\ref{sec:design}).
  \item We engineer a stateless hardware-conscious execution engine integrating $4096$\,B single-page vertex co-location, variance-stabilized 256-bit beam filtering, 3-to-2 carry-save {AVX2} popcounts, L1-bounded \textsc{Stage-0.5}/\textsc{CJ-Gate} refinement, and FP64 {SIMD} geodesic reranking (\S\ref{sec:system}).
  \item Across eight real and synthetic hyperbolic benchmarks on {AMD Zen~4}, \rquiver{} accelerates 32-thread graph construction by $5.44\times$ ($35.0$\,s vs.\ $190.2$\,s on {Cohere-1M}), cuts logical hot navigation memory by $2.60\times$ ($2.44\times$ page-co-located) versus 8-bit polar quantization, delivers $4.30\times$ (1T) to $6.31\times$ (24T) higher $99\%$-recall QPS on {Cohere-1M} (reaching $99.70\%$ peak Recall@10 vs.\ $96.78\%$ for {HyperspaceDB} and $98.91\%$ / $98.69\%$ for centered {Faiss} / {Milvus}, with $17.0\times$--$28.3\times$ higher QPS than {HyperspaceDB} at $95\%$ recall and $22.9\times$--$58.2\times$ over centered {Faiss} / {Milvus} at $98\%$ recall), reaches $53{,}483$\,QPS on {MERU-1M} ({ImageNet}, $3.37\times$--$3.88\times$ over {HyperspaceDB} FP32 at $99\%$ recall), $14{,}731$\,QPS on 1536D {OpenAI-1536} ({DBpedia}), and $65{,}405$\,QPS on {WordNet-Hazy}, and rescues tight boundary clusters from $34.2\%$ to $>99.4\%$ Recall@10 ($1.84\times$--$4.56\times$ higher $99\%$-recall QPS than {HyperspaceDB} FP32) while characterizing a configuration-dependent recall weakness on {Uniform-Narrow} via a four-stage telescoping audit and cross-system measurements (\S\ref{sec:eval}).
\end{itemize}
