Figure~\ref{fig:geometry_overview} summarizes the geometry and representation. Guided by Theorems~\ref{thm:conformal_flip}--\ref{thm:tangent_cone} and Proposition~\ref{prop:geodesic_apex}, \rquiver{} models the index as a \emph{warped-product fiber-bundle graph} over $\R_{\ge 0} \times_{\sinh} \Sunit$, decoupling exact radial curvature from 2-bit spherical directions in memory and separating spherical diversity edges from radial transitions during construction.

\subsection{Zero-Overhead Polar Vertex Representation}
\label{subsec:polar_layout}

\paragraph{32-Byte \texttt{PolarNodeMeta} Header.}
For each vertex $v \in \{0, \dots, N-1\}$, \rquiver{} stores a fixed 32-byte polar header (half a 64-byte cache line) paired with packed 2-bit {SM} bitplanes occupying $2\lceil D / 64\rceil \times 8$ bytes:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Unquantized radial scalars (12\,B):} Euclidean radius $r_v \in [0, 1/\sqrt{c})$, boundary factor $F_v=(1-cr_v^2)^{-1}=f_v/2$, and dimensionless geodesic depth $\rho_v = 2\atanh(\sqrt{c}\,r_v)$, computed in FP64 at ingestion and stored in FP32.
  \item \emph{Conditional-mean two-level {SM} scalars (12\,B):} weak-coordinate mean $a_v$, level step $\delta_v = b_v - a_v$, and reconstructed two-level norm $\sigma_v = \|\hat{u}_v\|_2$ in FP32 (Lemma~\ref{lem:sm_pythagorean}).
  \item \emph{Bitpacked count/cone tag and axial/norm scalar (8\,B):} a 32-bit word packing the 20-bit strong-magnitude count $s_v$ with a 12-bit residual cone tag ($\text{cone\_id}$, with \texttt{0x0FFF} denoting global native {SM}), paired with an FP32 field storing axial alignment $\alpha_v = \langle u_v, \mu_c\rangle$ for residual-cone vertices or precomputed canonical inverse norm $(D+3s_v)^{-1/2}$ for global vertices. The triple $(a_v,\delta_v,\sigma_v)$ supports the main conditional-mean hop scorer and \textsc{Stage-0.5}. The global inverse-norm slot supports legacy canonical paths, while residual scoring reads $\alpha_v$ and computes $\beta_v$.
\end{itemize}
At $D=768$, $M_0=32$, the co-located 2-bit record and 32 active edges occupy $384$\,B per vertex ($352$\,B without the sketch). Table~\ref{tab:node_layout} gives this logical payload. Table~\ref{tab:memory_audit} includes reserved edges, router arrays and the cold store. The active hot arrays occupy $517$\,MB for the page layout, $1{,}069$\,MB for {Polar-SQ8}, and $3{,}373$\,MB for {Polar-FP32}. Appendix~\ref{app:scalar_rounding} quantifies FP32 scalar rounding.

\paragraph{Purity-Gated \texttt{ResidualConeDict}.}
To realize Theorem~\ref{thm:tangent_cone}(3) without disturbing unclustered manifolds, \rquiver{} executes a deterministic cone-purity check at index build over a uniform sample of $\min(N, 8{,}192)$ vertices:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{Conformal depth gate:} If the 90th-percentile sample depth satisfies $p_{90}(\rho) < 1.0$, boundary conformal amplification is benign and residual encoding exits immediately with $0$ cones.
  \item \emph{Leader seeding and two-round purity refinement:} Otherwise, a deterministic spherical leader pass collects candidate unit axes separated by cosine $< \tau_{\mathrm{lead}} = 0.91$ (capped at $1{,}024$ candidates), followed by two rounds of spherical mean refinement assigning samples with $\langle u_s, \mu_c\rangle \ge \alpha_{\min} = 0.94$.
  A cone $\mu_c$ is retained if and only if it meets minimum sample support $\operatorname{clamp}(\lfloor N_{\mathrm{samp}}/1024\rfloor, 3, 12)$ and achieves high intra-cone mean alignment $\bar{\alpha}_c \ge 0.955$.
\end{enumerate}
Every database vertex $v$ achieving $\alpha_v = \langle u_v, \mu_c\rangle \ge 0.94$ against its nearest retained axis stores the 2-bit {SM} code of its normalized tangent residual $\tilde{r}_v = (u_v - \alpha_v\mu_c)/\sqrt{1 - \alpha_v^2}$ in the existing $16\lceil D/64\rceil$-byte bitplane slot and records $\text{cone\_id}$ in the 12-bit header field (with \texttt{0x0FFF} reserved for global {SM}, \textbf{0\,B/vertex} overhead), giving $\beta_v^2=1-\alpha_v^2\le0.1164$. The cosine-error multiplier is $\beta_{q,c}\beta_v$ in Theorem~\ref{thm:tangent_cone}(3).
On the four primary real datasets the dictionary stays empty. {MERU-1M} and {WordNet-Hazy} exit at the depth check ($p_{90}(\rho)=0.26$ and $0.78$). {Cohere-1M} and {OpenAI-1536} pass that check but have insufficient directional concentration. Both clustered synthetic sets retain 256 axes ($0.50$\,MiB at $D=512$). The deeper native {HyperE WordNet} embedding retains 33 axes (\S\ref{subsec:eval_deep}).

\subsection{Inside-Out Seeding and Stem-Excess QuadriPrune}
\label{subsec:compass}

By Theorem~\ref{thm:vamana_pathologies}(1), single-metric {Vamana} pruning allows interior vertices to occlude entire spherical neighborhoods of boundary vertices.
Algorithm~\ref{alg:compass_build} replaces isotropic pruning with an inside-out fiber-bundle construction.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Warped-Product Construction \& Chord-Swap Repair}
\label{alg:compass_build}
\small
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require Dataset $\mathcal{X} \subset \Bball$, max out-degree $M_0 = 2M$, build beam $L_c$, slack $\alpha > 1$
\Ensure Graph $G=(V,E)$ with maximum degree $M_0$ and measured entry-reachable set $\mathcal R$
\State Compute FP32 polar headers $(r_v, f_v, \rho_v)$, fit purity-gated \texttt{ResidualConeDict}, encode 2-bit {SM} payloads, and build \texttt{PolarRouter} (\S\ref{subsec:router})
\State Sort vertices $\pi = (v_1, \dots, v_N)$ in non-decreasing geodesic depth $\rho_{v_1} \le \dots \le \rho_{v_N}$ and initialize empty adjacency lists
\For{consecutive radial wave $B_t$ of up to 256 vertices in $\pi$}
  \For{each $u \in B_t$ in parallel}
    \State $\mathcal{C}_u \gets \textsc{PolarBeamSearch}(G, u, L_c)$ via 2-bit polar proxy $\widehat{P}_{\mathbb{B}}$
    \State Partition up to $M_0$ outgoing edges from $\mathcal{C}_u$ via \textbf{QuadriPrune}:
    \State \hspace{0.8em}(a) \emph{Geodesic fiber} (cumulative cap $\max(1,\lfloor3M_0/8\rfloor)$): stem-excess prune~\eqref{eq:dom_geo}
    \State \hspace{0.8em}(b) \emph{Shielded isobathic ring} (cumulative cap $\lfloor7M_0/8\rfloor$): rank by angle plus depth gap, test dominators with $|\rho_w-\rho_v|\le0.60$
    \State \hspace{0.8em}(c)--(d) \emph{Inward \& outward elevators} : split remaining capacity, select step-aligned transitions via~\eqref{eq:elevator_obj}, then apply fallback fill
    \State Insert reverse edges $v \to u$ for $v \in N^+(u)$, re-applying {QuadriPrune} when $\deg^+(v) > M_0$
  \EndFor
\EndFor
\State Final-prune every adjacency list to $M_0$ slots
\State $(\mathcal{R}, \texttt{parent}) \gets \textsc{DirectedBFSTree}(G, \text{entry\_hub})$ \Comment{\S\ref{subsec:repair}}
\For{each $u \in \pi$ with $u \notin \mathcal{R}$}
  \State Search $N^+(u)$, hubs, and the 256-vertex interior prefix for reachable $p$ with a free slot or removable non-tree chord
  \If{no qualifying anchor exists} \State Continue to the next vertex \EndIf
  \If{$\deg^+(p) < M_0$} append $p \to u$
  \Else\ replace peripheral chord $p \to w^\star$ s.t.\ $\texttt{parent}[w^\star] \ne p \land \deg^-(w^\star) \ge 2$ with $p \to u$
  \EndIf
  \State $\texttt{parent}[u] \gets p$; expand $(\mathcal{R}, \texttt{parent})$ via incremental {BFS} from $u$
\EndFor
\State \Return fixed-stride graph $G$, reachable set $\mathcal R$, and $4096$\,B \texttt{HotSmPage} layout
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\paragraph{Inside-Out Geodesic Seeding.}
Sorting vertices by non-decreasing depth schedules interior vertices before boundary waves. Router seeds are computed from the full database before these 256-vertex waves connect their adjacency lists.

\paragraph{Stem-Excess and Angle-Guarded Geodesic Dominance.}
To prevent interior vertices from occluding opposite subtrees via Theorem~\ref{thm:vamana_pathologies}(1)---a curvature-induced hub pathology distinct from flat angular redundancy~\cite{fu2019nsg,fu2022high} and cross-modal query shift~\cite{chen2024roargraph}---\rquiver{} replaces raw $\alpha$-pruning with \emph{stem-excess angle-guarded dominance}.
Let $\tilde{d}(x, y) = \sqrt{c}\,d_{\mathbb{B}}(x, y)$ denote dimensionless geodesic distance and $\ell_\angle(x, y) = 1 - \hat{\gamma}(u_x, u_y)$ denote angular loss.
Define the radial stem excess and effective non-stem distance of candidate $v$ relative to base $u$ and existing neighbor $w$:
\begin{align}
  \Delta_{\mathrm{stem}}(u, v; w) &\triangleq \max\!\bigl(0,\; |\rho_u - \rho_v| - |\rho_w - \rho_v|\bigr), \nonumber \\
  \tilde{d}_{\mathrm{eff}}(u, v; w) &\triangleq \max\!\bigl(0,\; \tilde{d}(u, v) - \Delta_{\mathrm{stem}}(u, v; w)\bigr).
  \label{eq:stem_excess}
\end{align}
Neighbor $w$ geodesically dominates candidate $v$ ($\operatorname{Dom}_{\mathrm{geo}}(w \mid u, v)$) if and only if
\begin{equation}
  \bigl[\ell_\angle(w, v) \le \ell_\angle(u, v)\bigr] \;\land\;
  \bigl[\alpha\,\tilde{d}(w, v) \le \tilde{d}_{\mathrm{eff}}(u, v; w)\bigr].
  \label{eq:dom_geo}
\end{equation}
Condition $\ell_\angle(w, v) \le \ell_\angle(u, v)$ forbids $w$ from pruning candidates at wider spherical angles than $u$ itself (preventing $180^\circ$ cross-origin occlusion), while subtracting $\Delta_{\mathrm{stem}}$ ensures $w$ shadows $v$ through genuine angular proximity rather than radial depth advantage alone.
Moreover, because $\tilde{d}(w, v) \ge |\rho_w - \rho_v|$ by the reverse triangle inequality, checking $\alpha|\rho_w - \rho_v| > \tilde{d}_{\mathrm{eff}}(u, v; w)$ rejects non-dominating pairs in $O(1)$ scalar arithmetic without evaluating $\arccosh$.

\paragraph{Shielded Isobathic Rings, Elevators, and Channel Allocation.}
Selection uses cumulative caps within each $M_0=32$-slot list.
(i)~The geodesic channel fills up to $\max(1,\lfloor3M_0/8\rfloor)=12$ slots via~\eqref{eq:dom_geo}.
(ii)~The ring channel extends the combined list to 28 entries. It orders candidates by $\ell_\angle(u,v)+0.85|\rho_u-\rho_v|$. A selected neighbor $w$ may dominate $v$ only if $|\rho_w-\rho_v|\le0.60$ and $\alpha\ell_\angle(w,v)\le\ell_\angle(u,v)$.
(iii)--(iv)~With $s$ entries selected and $h=M_0-s$ slots remaining, the inward pass targets $s+\lfloor h/2\rfloor$ total entries and the outward pass fills toward $M_0$. Thus a full 28-entry prefix leaves two slots for each radial direction. Their objective is
\begin{equation}
 J_{\mathrm{elev}}(u,v)=\ell_\angle(u,v)
 +0.15\bigl||\rho_v-\rho_u|-0.65\bigr|.
 \label{eq:elevator_obj}
\end{equation}
Candidates must satisfy $\rho_v<\rho_u-0.18$ inward or $\rho_v>\rho_u+0.18$ outward. Direction-specific dominators satisfy $\rho_w<\rho_u-0.10$ or $\rho_w>\rho_u+0.10$, respectively, and use the angular $\alpha$ test.
If these passes leave capacity, a diversity pass orders remaining candidates by $\ell_\angle(u,v)+0.25|\rho_u-\rho_v|$ and admits angular dominators within $|\rho_w-\rho_v|\le1.0$. A final ordered fill takes remaining distinct candidates up to $M_0$.
At constant depth the elevator sets are empty and the ring ranks by angle, using the fixed dataset-independent channel quotas and tolerances in Table~\ref{tab:fixed_parameters}. Appendix~\ref{app:vamana_pathologies} derives the channel-wise constant-depth and flat-curvature limits.

\subsection{Spanning-Tree Chord-Swap Island Repair}
\label{subsec:repair}

Two unrepaired {Cohere-1M} builds left 521 and 628 vertices with zero in-degree. Directed {BFS} from the entry hub missed 530 and 634 vertices, respectively.
The chord-swap pass in Algorithm~\ref{alg:compass_build} grows directed {BFS} entry reachability while preserving maximum out-degree $\deg^+(p)\le M_0$. A qualifying anchor has spare capacity or an eligible non-tree chord.
Directed {BFS} initializes a spanning tree $(\mathcal{R},\texttt{parent})$ from the router entry hub, each unreachable vertex $u \notin \mathcal{R}$ selects a reachable anchor $p \in \mathcal{R}$ (scanning $N^+(u)$, router hubs, and the interior core prefix) and either appends $p \to u$ (if $\deg^+(p) < M_0$) or replaces a peripheral non-tree chord $p \to w^\star$ satisfying both $\texttt{parent}[w^\star] \ne p$ and $\deg^-(w^\star) \ge 2$, decrementing $\deg^-(w^\star)$ in place and expanding $(\mathcal{R}, \texttt{parent})$ incrementally from $u$\ifpreprint\ (monotonic reachability invariant proved in Appendix~\ref{app:vamana_pathologies})\fi.
Because the pass preserves every spanning-tree edge, $\mathcal{R}$ grows monotonically. After repair, every vertex had positive in-degree on all 21 Core builds in the three-seed sweep. Directed {BFS} from the entry hub reached all $990{,}000$ on each {OpenAI-1536} build and all $1{,}000{,}000$ on each of the other 18 builds.

\subsection{Convex-Support Polar Router}
\label{subsec:router}

\rquiver{} builds $C=\min(N,\operatorname{clamp}(\lfloor N/100\rfloor,64,2048))$ directional landmarks in SM mode and crosses them with eight quantile-adaptive shells. At 1M scale this gives 2,048 fine cones and 16,384 cells, each storing up to eight 32-bit vertex IDs. For $C>256$, 64 coarse landmarks each retain a 128-cone query shortlist.
The query scores coarse landmarks, expands the leading shortlists, and uses the constrained radial minimizer $\rho_{\mathrm{sup}}^*(\rho_q,\gamma)$ from Proposition~\ref{prop:geodesic_apex}(2) to select shell representatives. The radial support endpoints are the smallest positive sampled database depth and the maximum depth. The variance-stabilized configuration activates a sampled isotropy guard that scans fine cones directly when coarse alignment falls below its dimension-scaled threshold. With validation queries, the entry policy can be tuned jointly with $(L,B)$ at each thread count. The hub-only policy skips the cone search and seeds from the origin hubs.
