Figure~\ref{fig:execution_overview} follows a query from page translation to final reranking. On million-scale hyperbolic graphs, achieving $99\%$ Recall@10 at beam width $L \in [128, 256]$ expands $300\text{--}350$ vertices and evaluates $4{,}500\text{--}5{,}800$ candidate neighbors per query, while reading only $B \in [64, 128]$ cold FP32 vectors during final reranking.
End-to-end latency is therefore governed by random-access {DTLB} walks, cache-line traffic during neighbor expansion, and 2-bit bitplane population counts.
Query-private scratch buffers are reset by generation stamps and reused across requests. Candidate scoring depends on the current query and index state.

\subsection{Single-Page Co-Location Avoiding Cross-Array DTLB Walks}
\label{subsec:colocated_page}

In conventional Structure-of-Arrays ({SoA}) engines, polar headers ($32$\,MB at $N=10^6$) and 2-bit bitplanes ($192$\,MB at $D=768$) occupy separate contiguous arrays hundreds of megabytes apart in virtual memory (whereas disk-block reorderers~\cite{wang2024starling} and neighbor-embedded quantizers~\cite{gou2025symphonyqg} either require graph-isomorphism renumbering or duplicate codes across edges).
Because x86-64 cores provide only $64\text{--}96$ L1 {DTLB} entries and $1{,}024\text{--}3{,}072$ L2 {DTLB} entries for $4$\,KiB pages, $5{,}500$ random neighbor hops across a $224$\,MB working set miss the {DTLB} on nearly every hop---forcing two independent page-table walks per candidate under {SoA} (one for metadata, a second for bitplanes).

Table~\ref{tab:hot_sm_page} shows our page-co-located \texttt{HotSmPage} layout:
each vertex occupies a power-of-two record of $S_{\mathrm{rec}} = \operatorname{next\_pow2}(64 + 16\lceil D/64\rceil)$ bytes ($256$\,B at $D=512, 768$ packing $K = 16$ vertices/page; $512$\,B at $D=1536$ packing $K = 8$ vertices/page; $128$\,B at $D=20$ packing $K = 32$ vertices/page, supporting up to $D = 16{,}128$ within one $4$\,KiB page).
Because $S_{\mathrm{rec}}$ divides $4096$ evenly ($\text{page\_id} = \text{vid} / K$, $\text{slot} = \text{vid} \bmod K$), every vertex's 64-byte \texttt{HotSmHeader} (\texttt{PolarNodeMeta} + \texttt{SymSketch256} in cache line~0) and its complete 2-bit {SM} bitplanes reside contiguously inside a single $4096$-byte OS page.
Once hardware resolves the virtual-to-physical translation for \texttt{HotSmHeader} at $\text{base} + S_{\mathrm{rec}} \cdot \text{slot}$ (supported up to $D \le 16{,}128$), any subsequent bitplane reads (\texttt{0x40..0xFF} at $D=768$) share the \emph{exact same $4$\,KiB virtual-page translation}, avoiding cross-array secondary page-table walks per accessed vertex record.

\subsection{Variance-Stabilized Two-Level Beam Filtering (\texttt{SymSketch256})}
\label{subsec:symsketch}

Even within one $4$\,KiB page, fetching full 2-bit {SM} bitplanes ($192$\,B = $3$ cache lines at $D=768$; $384$\,B = $6$ cache lines at $D=1536$) for every unvisited neighbor incurs unnecessary memory traffic once the beam tightens around $q$.
\rquiver{} first prefetches only the single 64-byte \texttt{HotSmHeader} cache line (consuming just one hardware Line Fill Buffer entry per neighbor) and executes a two-stage gate against the current $L$-th beam bound $\theta_{\mathrm{beam}}$:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Stage-1a (Exact Radial Lower-Bound Gate, $0$ extra cache lines):}
  Using $(r_v, f_v)$ from \texttt{HotSmHeader} (\texttt{0x00..0x1F}), if $P_{\mathrm{rad}}(r_q, r_v) = (r_q - r_v)^2 f_q f_v > \theta_{\mathrm{beam}}$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:geodesic_apex}(1)), $v$ is discarded by the lossless radial bound. \ifpreprint FP32 header perturbations follow Appendix~\ref{app:scalar_rounding}.\fi

  \item \textbf{Stage-1b (Binomial-Stabilized \texttt{SymSketch256} Gate, same $64$\,B header line):}
  The upper half of \texttt{HotSmHeader} (\texttt{0x20..0x3F}) stores a $256$-bit ($32$\,B) strided sign sketch.
  Computing the 256-bit Hamming distance $H \in \{0, \dots, 256\}$ against $q$'s sketch via four \texttt{u64} \texttt{XOR}+\texttt{POPCNT} instructions indexes two compile-time lookup tables ($2$\,KB total in L1): the exact arcsine table $\hat{\gamma}_{256} = \texttt{COS\_PI\_256}[H] = \cos(\pi H / 256)$ and the binomial-model standard-deviation ratio table $\rho_{\mathrm{std}}(H) = \texttt{SKETCH256\_STD\_RATIO}[H] = \max(1, \sigma_{\mathrm{sk}}(H/256) / \sigma_{\mathrm{sk}}(48/256))$ derived in Proposition~\ref{prop:asym_cj_gate}(3).
  Forming the variance-stabilized optimistic cosine bound $\hat{\gamma}_{\mathrm{upper}} = \min(1, \hat{\gamma}_{256} + \Delta_0 \cdot \rho_{\mathrm{std}}(H))$ yields optimistic proxy $\widehat{P}_{\mathbb{B}}^{\mathrm{opt}}(q, v) = R(r_q, r_v) + W(r_q, r_v)(1 - \hat{\gamma}_{\mathrm{upper}})$; whenever $\widehat{P}_{\mathbb{B}}^{\mathrm{opt}}(q, v) > \theta_{\mathrm{beam}}$ (for non-residual vertices once the beam fills), $v$ is pruned without prefetching or reading its trailing bitplane cache lines.
\end{enumerate}
Scaling the sketch margin by $\rho_{\mathrm{std}}(H)$ keeps tight filtering ($1.0\times$) for high-similarity neighbors ($H \le 48$) while automatically widening to $2.265\times$ at $H=115$ and a maximum of $2.306\times$ at $H=128$ to prevent premature pruning on moderate-angle manifolds.
In our controlled three-seed ablation on {Cohere-1M}, Stage-1a and \texttt{SymSketch256} skip $57.6\%$ of full bitplane loads, raising single-thread $99\%$-recall QPS from $1{,}752$ ({SoA}) to $1{,}909$ (\texttt{HotSmPage}) and $2{,}071$ (\texttt{HotSmPage} + \texttt{SymSketch256}, $+18.2\%$ total).

\subsection{3-to-2 Carry-Save AVX2 Bitplane Kernel}
\label{subsec:csa_kernel}

During hop traversal, the engine evaluates the conditional-mean product in Lemma~\ref{lem:sm_pythagorean}, followed by~\eqref{eq:sm_calibration}. For disagreement mask $x=s_q\oplus s_v$, the five counts are $\popcnt(x)$, $\popcnt(x\wedge m_q)$, $\popcnt(x\wedge m_v)$, $\popcnt(m_q\wedge m_v)$, and $\popcnt(x\wedge m_q\wedge m_v)$. Together with the two headers' $(a,\delta,\sigma,s)$, these counts reconstruct the bilinear product without square roots in the main scorer.

\paragraph{Residual-Aware Hop Dispatch.}
Let $K(a,b)$ denote this calibrated symmetric scorer and $A(q,b)$ the asymmetric scorer of \S\ref{subsec:stage05_rerank}. A global vertex uses $K(u_q,u_v)$. A residual vertex uses its cone tag to read axis $\mu_c$ and cache $\alpha_{q,c}=\langle u_q,\mu_c\rangle$, $\beta_{q,c}=\sqrt{1-\alpha_{q,c}^2}$. Its header stores $\alpha_v$ in the shared axial/norm slot and the conditional means of $\tilde r_v$.
\begin{algorithm}[t]
 \caption{Residual-aware candidate cosine scoring}
 \label{alg:residual_score}
 \begin{algorithmic}[1]
 \Require Header $h_v$, candidate bitplanes, query cache, phase
 \If{$h_v$ has a global cone tag}
  \State \Return $K(u_q,u_v)$ for hops, $A(u_q,u_v)$ for pool refinement
 \EndIf
 \State Read cached $(\alpha_{q,c},\beta_{q,c})$, computing one axis dot product on a miss
 \State $\alpha_v\gets h_v.\alpha$, $\beta_v\gets\sqrt{\max(0,1-\alpha_v^2)}$
 \If{candidate residual is degenerate}
  \State \Return $\alpha_{q,c}\alpha_v$
 \EndIf
 \If{a residual slot is cached, or $|\alpha_{q,c}|\ge0.20$, $\beta_{q,c}>10^{-6}$ and a free slot exists}
  \State Reuse or materialize unit $\tilde r_{q,c}$, its bitplanes, and SM parameters
  \State $t_{\mathrm{res}}\gets K(\tilde r_{q,c},\tilde r_v)$ for hops, $A(\tilde r_{q,c},\tilde r_v)$ for refinement
  \State \Return $\operatorname{clip}_{[-1,1]}(\alpha_{q,c}\alpha_v+\beta_{q,c}\beta_v t_{\mathrm{res}})$
 \Else
  \State $t_{\mathrm{uq}}\gets K(u_q,\tilde r_v)$ for hops, $A(u_q,\tilde r_v)$ for refinement \Comment{$\tilde r_v\perp\mu_c\Rightarrow\langle u_q,\tilde r_v\rangle=\beta_{q,c}\langle\tilde r_{q,c},\tilde r_v\rangle$}
  \State \Return $\operatorname{clip}_{[-1,1]}(\alpha_{q,c}\alpha_v+\beta_v t_{\mathrm{uq}})$
 \EndIf
 \end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
Each query caches projections for at most 512 dictionary axes and materializes at most 16 residual queries. A first cone visit reads one $D$-coordinate axis and performs $O(D)$ work. Materialization adds $O(D)$ re-orthogonalization and encoding, with $O(16D+512)$ thread-local storage. A query visiting every dictionary axis performs at most 512 axis projections and 16 residual encodings. Repeated visits reuse these transforms. Upper-layer scoring without a query cache uses the global-query/residual-candidate branch. The radial gate applies to both tags, and residual vertices proceed directly to the full bitplane scorer after that gate.

Invoking {AVX2} \texttt{vpshufb} nibble-lookup popcounts~\cite{mula2018faster} independently on every bitplane would execute $15$ \texttt{vpshufb} shuffle instructions per $768$D candidate.
Adapting bitwise carry-save reduction~\cite{mula2018faster} to {SM} bitplanes, \rquiver{} compresses triplets $(A, B, C)$ across 256-bit \texttt{YMM} registers via a \emph{3-to-2 Carry-Save Adder} ({CSA}):
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{Sum} = A \oplus B \oplus C, \qquad
  \mathrm{Carry} = (A \wedge B) \vee \bigl((A \oplus B) \wedge C\bigr),
  \label{eq:csa_identity}
\end{equation}
which satisfies $\popcnt(A) + \popcnt(B) + \popcnt(C) = \popcnt(\mathrm{Sum}) + 2\,\popcnt(\mathrm{Carry})$ identically\ifpreprint\ (verified bit-by-bit in Appendix~\ref{app:sm_algebra})\fi, cutting \texttt{vpshufb} shuffle instructions by $33\%$ ($15 \to 10$ per $768$D candidate) and evaluating each surviving candidate in $<35$\,ns alongside distance-2 software prefetching.

\subsection{L1-Bounded \textsc{Stage-0.5} Asymmetric Projection, \textsc{CJ-Gate}, and FP64 Reranking}
\label{subsec:stage05_rerank}

Once beam search converges to a pool $\mathcal{P}$ of size $L$, fetching all $L$ cold FP32 vectors ($L \times 3$\,KB) would dominate latency at large $L$.
\rquiver{} executes a three-step tail pipeline:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{L1-Bounded \textsc{Stage-0.5} Asymmetric Re-Scoring:}
  By Theorem~\ref{thm:conformal_flip}(2) and Proposition~\ref{prop:asym_cj_gate}(1), evaluating $\hat{\gamma}_{\mathrm{asym}}(u_q, u_v) = \operatorname{clip}_{[-1,1]}(\langle u_q, \hat{u}_v\rangle / \sigma_v^2)$ with unquantized $u_q$ and $(a_v, \delta_v, \sigma_v)$ already resident in \texttt{PolarNodeMeta} eliminates both cross-shell query-noise non-cancellation ($e_q = 0$) and norm-inflation bias.
  Let $S_{\mathrm{tot}} = \sum_{i=1}^D u_{q,i}$ and define three masked signed-query subset sums over candidate bitplanes $(s_v, m_v)$: positive-sign sum $S_+ = \sum_{i : s_{v,i} = 1} u_{q,i}$, strong-candidate sum $S_h = \sum_{i : m_{v,i} = 1} u_{q,i}$, and joint positive-strong sum $S_{+h} = \sum_{i : s_{v,i} = 1 \land m_{v,i} = 1} u_{q,i}$ (equivalently expressed via unsigned $|u_{q,i}|$ and sign-agreement masks $\mathbb{I}[s_{q,i}=s_{v,i}]$).
  Then $\langle u_q, \hat{u}_v\rangle$ reduces algebraically to
  \begin{equation}
    \langle u_q, \hat{u}_v\rangle = a_v\bigl(2 S_+ - S_{\mathrm{tot}}\bigr) + \delta_v\bigl(2 S_{+h} - S_h\bigr).
    \label{eq:asym_subset_sums}
  \end{equation}
  Unlike 4-bit nibble {SIMD} lookup tables~\cite{andre2015cache,andre2017accelerated,trent2024bbq}, \texttt{AsymQuerySm} encodes $u_q$ once per query into a $1.5$\,KiB L1-resident \texttt{i16} fixed-point array ($q_{\mathrm{i16},i} = \operatorname{round}(8100\,u_{q,i})$ at $D=768$; Cauchy--Schwarz guarantees 16-lane sums remain $\le32{,}408<2^{15}-1$ without overflow) and accumulates $(S_+, S_h, S_{+h})$ via register-resident {AVX2} 16-lane bit-mask selection and \texttt{vpmaddwd} (or an optional $2$\,KiB byte-mask {LUT}) exclusively over the $L$ pool members.
  For residual-cone pool members, \textsc{Stage-0.5} reuses the cone cache from Algorithm~\ref{alg:residual_score} and lazily builds a fixed-point query array for each materialized residual. It applies $A$ in the same materialized or global-query branch.

  \item \textbf{\textsc{CJ-Gate} Observable-Margin Plateau Expansion:}
  Sort $\mathcal P$ by asymmetric proxy and let $B_0=\min(B,L)$, $j_{\mathrm{ref}}=\min(k,B_0)$, $p_{\mathrm{ref}}=\widehat P_{(j_{\mathrm{ref}})}$. Compute one reference uncertainty $s_{\mathrm{ref}}$ from~\eqref{eq:cj_sigma}, using empirical $\kappa_{\mathrm{SM}}=0.221$ to target near-tie plateaus in the $\ge 99.5\%$ high-recall regime. A materialized cone uses attenuation $\beta_{q,c}\beta_v$, a global-query/residual branch uses $\beta_v$, and a global code uses 1.
  If $B_0<L$ and $\widehat P_{(B_0)}-p_{\mathrm{ref}}\le z s_{\mathrm{ref}}$, extend the rerank prefix while
  \begin{equation}
   \widehat P_{(j)}\le p_{\mathrm{ref}}+(z+0.8)s_{\mathrm{ref}},\qquad
   j\le\min(L,\max(B_0,B_{\max})).
  \end{equation}
  The measured full configuration fixes the relative-gap parameter at $0.22$, giving $z=\operatorname{clip}_{[0.5,4.5]}(0.22\cdot2/0.22)=2$ and $B_{\max}=768$. With no router metadata, the reference scale is $\max(p_{\mathrm{ref}},10^{-9})\cdot0.22$.

  \item \textbf{FP64 {SIMD} Geodesic Reranking:}
  Near $\|v\|_2 \to 1/\sqrt{c}$, single-precision evaluation of $1 - c\|v\|_2^2$ loses mantissa precision.
  \rquiver{} prefetches cold vector $v_{(j+2)}$ while widening $q$ and $v_{(j)}$ via \texttt{vcvtps2pd} into 8-wide {AVX-512F} (or 4-wide {AVX2+FMA}) FP64 accumulators, computing exact $P_{\mathbb{B}}(q, v_{(j)})$ in double precision and invoking $\asinh$~\eqref{eq:proxy_to_geodesic} only on the final $k = 10$ results.
\end{enumerate}
